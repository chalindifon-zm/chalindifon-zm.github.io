About me

I combine field surveying and geospatial analysis to turn location-based data into information that supports planning and decision-making.

My experience includes GIS data management, spatial data quality checks, GNSS surveying, site setting-out, terrain and land-cover analysis, and engineering survey support. I’m particularly interested in using GIS to address urban challenges such as flood risk and infrastructure planning.

My current academic project focuses on a GIS-based Flood Risk Decision Support System for municipal planning in Lusaka, with a detailed case study in Kanyama.
